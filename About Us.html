Our Home

We operate out of two halls — Central Park in Concord and Howse Park in Mortlake — welcoming youth and families from across the local area.

Our Group Colours

Our colours — river blue for the Parramatta River, sky blue for the open sky above our adventures, and wattle gold for the native bush that surrounds us — reflect the spirit of our home on the river and our deep connection to this part of Sydney.
