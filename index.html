Welcome to Cabo Scout Group

Based in Concord, CABO Scouts provides exciting outdoor adventures, leadership experiences, and lifelong friendships for youth aged 8–25.

Adventure Starts Here

1st Cabo Scout Group is one of the best troops in Sydney, providing youth in the Concord area with outdoor adventure, social skills, and life skills. Cabo Scouts is a safe place to have fun and learn with friends.

We invite youth aged 8–25 in the Cabarita and Mortlake area to join our exciting group. Our colours — river blue, sky and wattle gold — reflect the spirit of our home on the Parramatta River.
