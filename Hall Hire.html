Hall Hire

Our Scout Halls in Concord and Mortlake are available for community hire — perfect for celebrations, club meetings, fitness classes and more.

Managed by parent volunteers

Our Hall Hire booking is managed by parent volunteers. Response to enquiries may in some cases take a couple of days — thanks in advance for your patience.

Our Halls

We have two halls available to hire. Both are available for one-off events or regular ongoing bookings.

Scout Hall — Central Park

96/98 Wellbank St, Concord NSW 2137

The main hall has a polished wooden floor and measures approx 15m × 9m. There is a well-equipped kitchen and approximately 40 chairs and 8 trestle tables. The hall does not have its own toilet but a key to access the council-maintained toilet block next door is provided.

Scout Hall — Howse Park

Howse Park, Mortlake NSW

The main hall has a polished wooden floor and measures approx 9m × 9m. Features a newly renovated kitchen and approximately 40 chairs and 8 trestle tables. The hall has its own interior toilets and direct access to Howse Park, which contains a well-equipped children's playground.
